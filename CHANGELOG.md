# Changelog

## 3.1
- Pet conversations can no longer get stuck. A watchdog finishes turns that never get a reply, and chats where the pets just echo each other end early.
- Pets chat about random topics instead of the CPU/RAM.
- Pets build much more on their own, finish their own half-built projects, and celebrate (and cheer each other on) when something's done.

## 3.0
- Moods (happy / sad / angry) driven by chat, petting, throwing and events, with faces, tints, emotes and behaviour to match.
- Building: houses, towers, pyramids, forts, huts, igloos, crypts, campfires, gardens. Placing flowers, signs and items. Smashing, and recycling the rubble.
- Climbing your windows on sway / Hyprland (or via `windows_command`), walking on them and riding along when they move. Ceiling crawling.
- Ball, tag, dancing, thoughts, self-awareness in prompts, and memory between restarts.

## 2.0
- 8 pets, LLM chat (Ollama / OpenAI-compatible), pet-to-pet conversations, speech bubbles.

## 1.0
- First release: Tux, slime and zombie; walk, climb, sleep, drag and throw; custom pet packs.
