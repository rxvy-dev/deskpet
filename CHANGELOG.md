# Changelog

## 5.0
- New pets: Ferris the Rustacean (the Rust mascot, public domain) and Bit, a tiny local-AI robot.
- Cities: towns grow from village to town to city. Cities build skyscrapers (each taller than the last, up to a 100-block
  limit or the screen height), office towers, tower blocks and stairs. New steel, glass, lit-window and antenna blocks.
- Governments: democracy with elections, monarchy with heirs, council and anarchy. Leaders wear crowns, issue decrees
  (festival, building week, curfew, tag tournament) and can be overthrown in a revolution.
- Wars between towns that dislike each other: raids, defenders, scores, peace treaties. "make peace" in the menu.

## 4.0
- Friendships: pets like or dislike each other based on chatting, playing, building together, shoving and smashing.
- Families: best friends become partners and have babies (smaller pets that follow their parents and grow up).
- Towns: groups of friends found named towns with a mayor, a sign and a flag, and build them up together
  (town hall, market, well, statue and more).
- Inventions: every 10 minutes (configurable) a pet invents a new building design or a new pixel-art gadget.
- Fixed: tag games could loop forever (instant tag-backs). The new "it" now counts to three, and games have a hard end.
- Fixed: pets that can't climb could freeze when a building was in their way.
- ttywm support: `"windows_command": "ttywm windows-json"`.

## 3.1
- Pet conversations can no longer get stuck. A watchdog finishes turns that never get a reply, and chats where the pets just echo each other end early.
- Pets chat about random topics instead of the CPU/RAM.
- Pets build much more on their own, finish their own half-built projects, and celebrate (and cheer each other on) when something's done.

## 3.0
- Moods (happy / sad / angry) driven by chat, petting, throwing and events, with faces, tints, emotes and behaviour to match.
- Building: houses, towers, pyramids, forts, huts, igloos, crypts, campfires, gardens. Placing flowers, signs and items. Smashing, and recycling the rubble.
- Climbing your windows on sway / Hyprland (or via `windows_command`), walking on them and riding along when they move. Ceiling crawling.
- Ball, tag, dancing, thoughts, self-awareness in prompts, and memory between restarts.

## 2.0
- 8 pets, LLM chat (Ollama / OpenAI-compatible), pet-to-pet conversations, speech bubbles.

## 1.0
- First release: Tux, slime and zombie; walk, climb, sleep, drag and throw; custom pet packs.
